## The two gates that decide the paper

Do not skip these, and do not reorder them later.

**Gate 1 (end of Step 2): instrument validation.** If personas fail validation
badly, everything downstream inherits the failure. Fix it there, not in week 13.

**Gate 2 (Step 4): active beats random.** On the families the Step-1 report
identifies as having parameter-level headroom, the Probe Designer must beat random
probing. If it does not, diagnose before building anything else — that comparison
is the paper's core claim, and knowing in week 8 leaves time to reframe.

`04_EVALUATION.md` names three things never to de-scope: instrument validation,
the active-vs-random comparison, and the plausibility ablation (RQ4). Those three
*are* the paper.
